How does it work?

1. Our robots read Google Trends twice a day

The fastest-rising searches in Tunisia (over 24 h, 48 h and 7 days), as Google shows them: typed word, approximate volume, growth, related searches.

2. We keep everything, day after day

Google keeps only 7 days. We keep every trend with its date: that is how the new topics, the comebacks and the history are born.

3. Football is hidden by default

Sport crushes everything else in searches: the “Hide” bar at the top hides football (and, if you wish, other sports, entertainment, politics, weather). Tap a button to change; your choice is kept.

4. We sort by category and spot opportunities

Keywords in French, Arabic and Tunisian, then Google's categories; an opportunity = a useful sector + a buying intent or a large volume.
