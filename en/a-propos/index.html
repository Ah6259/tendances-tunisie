How does the site work?

What the site does, where the figures come from, what they mean and what they do not say.

Data read on ⁦11/11/2026⁩

What the site does

Number boxes

The four number boxes at the top of the home page are links: today, this week, new, history.

Today

The “Today” list shows the searches that started in the last 24 hours, strongest first, with their approximate volume and growth.

New

“New” are the words never seen in our readings before the last 24 hours: a topic that is starting to draw attention.

Unusual rises

“Unusual rises” are this week's searches with a growth of at least 500% or a volume of at least 5,000.

Opportunities

The “Opportunities” page keeps the rising searches of a sector useful to entrepreneurs that carry an intent (price, buy, rent…) or a large volume.

Categories

Every trend is filed in a category (real estate, cars, jobs, education, tourism, health, shopping, technology, government, energy, money, sport, entertainment, weather, politics, religion, other) and every category has its page.

Typed words

The “Typed words” page collects every week the phrases Google suggests to Tunisians when they start typing, with the date they appeared and disappeared.

History

The “History” page keeps everything since the first reading: trends by day, by week and by month, with the dominant categories.

A page for every word

Every word has its page: every time it rose, its volume, its related searches, its category and a link to its curve on Google Trends.

Fading, coming back

“Fading” = present last week, absent this week; “coming back” = seen on at least 3 different days this month.

Hide football (and other categories)

The “Hide” bar above the rankings hides football by default and, if you wish, other sports, entertainment, politics and weather; your choice is kept on your device, ranks are renumbered, and a category page always shows its own category.

Filters

Lists can be filtered by category and by word, show 10 rows then “Show N more”.

Three languages

The site exists in French, Arabic and English, each language at its own address (buttons “ع / Fr / En” at the top).

Share

The round “Share” button at the top sends the page link through the phone's share menu, otherwise through WhatsApp.

Your feedback

The “Your feedback” section at the bottom of the home page sends your message to the site's creator (e-mail required so we can reply).

Open data

Our readings are published as open data (donnees/analyse.json): our own dated and categorised readings, with the source cited, not a copy of Google.

Automatic updates

Everything is automatic: two readings a day (trends) and one a week (typed words) by our robots, without human intervention; the reading date is at the top of every page.

Where the figures come from

Google Trends, Tunisia: the list of fastest-rising searches (4 h, 24 h, 48 h and 7 days), read twice a day, exactly like the public page trends.google.com/trending?geo=TN, with its official RSS feed as a backup. Google search suggestions (Tunisia): the phrases suggested when you start typing, collected every week.

We read these pages with an identified robot, without bypassing any protection, and we cite the source on every page as Google asks: Data source: Google Trends.

What the figures mean (and do not say)

  • Volume: an order of magnitude given by Google in tiers (200+, 1,000+, 5,000+…), never an exact number. We do not add it up between words.
  • Growth: the rise in % computed by Google over the period (capped at 1,000%).
  • Rises only: Google Trends shows what is rising, not the absolute ranking (“facebook” or “youtube” never appear: too stable).
  • No regions: Google does not give trends by governorate for Tunisia.
  • Two trends can be compared only over the same period and the same reading date.
  • Categories: decided by our keywords (French, Arabic, Tunisian) then by Google's categories; a misfiled word can be reported to us (“Your feedback”).

Open data

Our readings can be reused freely with the credit “Search trends in Tunisia (tendances.clicvia.com), based on Google Trends”: donnees/analyse.json (today's analysis) and donnees/tendances.json (every trend seen, dated and categorised). These are our readings, not a copy of Google's services.

Who is behind this site

An independent site from the Clicvia family (free services for Tunisia), unrelated to Google. It is fully automatic. Brands mentioned in the trends belong to their owners.
